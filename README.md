### 🏥 Clinical Authority meets Data Science & Machine Learning
I am a **Senior Healthcare Data Analyst & Health Informatics Specialist** with a dual background: years of **hands-on clinical experience as a surgeon** combined with advanced expertise in **Healthcare Business Intelligence, Predictive Analytics, and Deep Learning**. 

My core focus is building production-ready pipelines for **Medical Image Analysis (DICOM/CT)**, clinical workflow optimization, and actionable healthcare dashboards.

---

### 🛠️ Technical Stack & Domain Expertise

- **Languages & Frameworks:** Python (Pandas, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, OpenCV), SQL, FastAPI
- **Data Engineering & BI:** Google BigQuery, PostgreSQL, Power BI, Tableau, Docker
- **Healthcare & Informatics:** DICOM/CT Scan Processing, HL7 FHIR Interoperability, Clinical KPI Optimization
- **ML & AI:** Medical Computer Vision, Explainable AI (SHAP), Predictive Patient Risk Modeling

### 🚀 High-Impact Healthcare Projects

- 📁 **[Medical Imaging & CT Scan Analysis Pipeline]**  
  *Python, PyTorch, SimpleITK, Streamlit*  
  End-to-end computer vision pipeline for processing DICOM datasets, applying Hounsfield Unit scaling, and running slice-level anomaly detection.

- 📁 **[Clinical Dashboard & Operational Performance Suite]**  
  *SQL, Google BigQuery, Power BI*  
  Data warehouse schema and interactive executive dashboards tracking ER wait times, readmission rates, and hospital capacity.

- 📁 **[EHR Patient Readmission Predictor]**  
  *Python, XGBoost, SHAP, Scikit-learn*
